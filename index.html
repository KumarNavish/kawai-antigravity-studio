Connecting Your Kawai CA-701 to Mac

Method 1: USB-to-Host Cable (Recommended)

1. Connect a standard USB-B to USB-C (or USB-A) cable from the USB to Host port on the CA-701 directly into your Mac.

2. Ensure the Kawai CA-701 power is turned ON.

3. Click "Connect MIDI" on the top bar (or refresh the page). It will connect automatically.

Method 2: Bluetooth MIDI

1. On Kawai CA-701 touchscreen: Go to Menu → Bluetooth → Bluetooth MIDI = ON.

2. On your Mac: Open Audio MIDI Setup (Cmd+Space → type "Audio MIDI Setup").

3. In the menu bar: Select Window → Show MIDI Studio.

4. Double-click the Bluetooth icon in the toolbar, find CA701, and click Connect.

5. Return to this browser window and click "Connect MIDI".

Method 3: Browser Permissions (Chrome / Brave)

Click the tune icon / lock icon next to localhost:4321 in your browser address bar and verify that MIDI devices is set to Allow.
